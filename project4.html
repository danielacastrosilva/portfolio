Project 04 — Behind the Scenes

A UX-Driven Approach to Gaming Community Design

Step 01

Research & Direction Alignment

The process started with close collaboration with the team behind the gaming hub Press Start. I gathered requirements directly from stakeholders, including the project lead, and aligned the direction with the existing brand book and design guidelines together with the internal design team.

To ensure strategic positioning within the market, I analysed and compared conventions across the gaming industry. This helped define what the platform needed to communicate, and how it could differentiate itself in a crowded space. A key focus emerged early on: strong CTAs, a simplified layout, and clear presentation of core information on the homepage.

Deliverable Competitor Mapping
Step 02

Ideation & Wireframing

Using Figma, I translated the research insights into initial low-fidelity wireframes focused on hierarchy and content structure. Since visual direction, colors, and imagery were already defined, the work concentrated on layout logic and information architecture rather than styling.

The wireframes were continuously iterated and refined into a more structured mid-fidelity direction, where components and layout systems started to reflect realistic usage scenarios and user flows.

As the concepts evolved, menus, imagery, and layout patterns began to form a cohesive system. The ideation phase wasn’t just about generating ideas, it was about shaping a direction that balances usability, aesthetics, and technical reality.

Deliverable Low-fidelity wireframes
Step 03

Step 3 · High-Fidelity Design & Testing

After internal alignment, I developed a high-fidelity prototype in Figma, including interactive elements such as navigation states and button behaviours.

The prototype was tested with both target users and external participants to validate clarity and usability. The feedback confirmed the overall structure, but also revealed issues in copywriting that caused confusion around the platform’s purpose. These insights were documented and shared with stakeholders to highlight the importance of clear messaging alongside visual design.

Deliverable High-Fidelity web concept prototype, User testing reports
Step 04

Final Result

The final deliverable was a high-fidelity, interactive UX prototype supported by user testing insights and clear design rationale. It demonstrates a cohesive information hierarchy, scalable layout system, and a user flow designed for both newcomers and experienced users.

The prototype is fully prepared for implementation within WordPress, respecting both technical constraints and editorial workflows. It gives the web team a clear, actionable blueprint for bringing the redesign to life without guesswork.

The solution is built to integrate seamlessly into a CMS-driven environment or custom implementation, ensuring flexibility for development. The structure and UX decisions are already informing the ongoing development of the live website, which is currently under construction.
