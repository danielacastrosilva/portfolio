Process Deep Dive

Behind the Scenes

From the first brief to the final deliverable — a transparent look at how the work is made.

Step 01

Discovery & Research

Every project starts with a period of deep listening. I conduct stakeholder interviews, competitive landscape analysis, and — where applicable — user research sessions. The goal is to arrive at a precise problem statement that can guide every subsequent decision.

During this phase I produce a research synthesis document: a single source of truth that maps insights, identifies tensions, and proposes design principles specific to the project. Nothing moves forward without alignment on these foundations.

Duration 1–2 weeks
Deliverable Research synthesis, design principles
Step 02

Concept & Strategy

With research synthesised, the concept phase is about finding the single idea that can anchor the entire project. I explore multiple strategic directions through moodboards, reference collections, and written concept statements before committing to a direction with the client.

Information architecture is mapped at this stage — sitemaps, user flows, and content models. The skeleton must be sound before the surface is considered.

Duration 1–3 weeks
Deliverable Moodboards, IA maps, concept statement
Step 03

Design & Iteration

Design happens in rounds. The first round is exploratory — bold, fast, and deliberately imperfect. The second round is where judgment enters. By the third round, every decision has a reason and every pixel has earned its place.

I share work early and often. Surprises at the end of a project are a failure of process. I use Figma for interactive prototyping so stakeholders experience the work as users will — in motion, not on a static PDF.

Duration 3–6 weeks
Deliverable Wireframes, UI designs, interactive prototypes
Step 04

Final Result & Delivery

The final deliverable is never just files. It is a coherent system with thorough documentation — a design handbook, component library, usage guidelines, and in many cases, a recorded walkthrough for the development team.

I remain available during development handoff to answer questions, review implementation, and course-correct where needed. The design is only as good as the experience that reaches the user.

Deliverables Design system, assets, documentation
Support 30-day dev handoff support included
