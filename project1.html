Project 01 — Behind the Scenes

Strengthened community engagement at Senior Centers. From initial research to final implementation.

Step 01

Community Research

Strong service design starts in the real world, not in assumptions. To understand why communication between Huddinge kommun and local seniors wasn’t reaching its audience, I conducted on-site community research through conversations with both staff and seniors in the area. The goal was to identify where the experience was breaking down across physical and digital touchpoints.

To deepen the research, I carried out a Service Safari and experienced the service from the users’ perspective. This helped uncover friction points that traditional stakeholder meetings often miss. The insights were clear. The issue wasn’t lack of communication, it was lack of accessible communication design.

Most information relied heavily on digital channels with low adoption among the target group. Printed materials existed, but lacked hierarchy, readability, and visual presence. Flyers used dense layouts and small typography, while posters failed to create enough visibility in the physical environment. Across the area, there were almost no strong offline touchpoints guiding seniors toward the service. The information existed. The design strategy didn’t..

Step 02

Ideation & Design Direction

With the research insights established, the next phase focused on turning observations into actionable design opportunities. The concepts were developed within the organization’s existing framework and resources, ensuring the solutions stayed both realistic and scalable.

My ideation process is intentionally broad in the beginning. I explore multiple directions, challenge expected solutions, and allow ideas to evolve before narrowing down the strongest concept. That balance between strategy and experimentation is where the most relevant solutions emerge.

Instead of reinventing the system, the direction became clear: amplify what already exists. By working with the existing brand guidelines and communication material, I focused on improving clarity, accessibility, and visual presence through stronger hierarchy and more intentional graphic communication. My background in graphic design and brand systems became a key part of translating insights into tangible design decisions.

Deliverable Service blueprint mapping, Design directions
Step 03

Design Execution & Visual System

With the direction established, the focus shifted into visual execution and production. Using Adobe Creative Suite, primarily Adobe InDesign, I designed communication assets tailored to the organization’s existing formats and real-world touchpoints.

The layouts were created around practical distribution needs. A4 formats were designed for quick and accessible information sharing across indoor and outdoor notice boards, while larger 50×70 cm compositions were developed for A-stand signage to create stronger visibility in public space. I also designed branded coffee punch cards, turning an already important social ritual within the senior community into an engaging and recognizable touchpoint. To expand the service beyond physical communication, I created a low-fidelity homepage concept for the Seniorträff website experience.

Deliverable Low-Fidelity web concept prototype, Offline touchpoint concepts
Step 04

Final Result & Concept Delivery

The project was delivered within a four-week timeframe as a fully developed concept designed for long-term use and future implementation. The final output was a concept presentation that brought together research, ideation, and design decisions into a clear and actionable direction. It focused on communicating not just the solution itself, but the reasoning behind it and its potential impact on real-world communication challenges.

The work positioned the solution as a scalable communication system for Huddinge kommun, focusing on improving accessibility, visibility, and consistency across physical and digital touchpoints. The delivery included a concrete design direction with defined applications across formats, making the concept ready to be taken further into implementation and iteration in a real-world context.
