Huddinge kommun

Strengthening community by boosting engagement at Senior Centers

UX, SERVICE DESIGN

A redesigned activity schedule takes the lead position on the Senior Center webpage, making programs instantly visible and significantly improving discoverability, engagement, and overall awareness of what the center offers.

Immanuelskyrkan

User‑centered design for a diverse and growing church community

UX DESIGN

A redesigned website puts the needs of Immanuel Church’s visitors and members at the center, making it easier for people of all ages and backgrounds to find what they’re looking for. Clear navigation, simplified language, and structured content help users reach their goals without friction, whether they’re exploring faith, looking for events, or seeking support.

Tailored pathways guide key groups, seniors, youth, and Korean‑speaking members, to the information most relevant to them. By reducing cognitive load and creating culturally and age‑appropriate entry points, the experience becomes more intuitive, welcoming, and inclusive for the church’s diverse community.

Improved content hierarchy and streamlined user flows make the website a more functional and intuitive digital experience. Members can now access practical information, while new visitors gain a clearer understanding of what the church offers and how to get involved.

How I Work

Process

01

Discovery

Every project begins with genuine curiosity. I listen — to stakeholders, to users, to the market. No assumptions, no templates. Understanding the specific problem before reaching for solutions.

02

Strategy

Design without strategy is decoration. I map information architecture, define success metrics, and frame the creative brief before a single pixel is placed. This is where clarity is earned.

03

Design

From rough sketches to refined systems. Iteration is not indecision — it is how great work is made. I design with intention, test with rigor, and refine until everything feels inevitable.

04

Delivery

Handoff is not the end of a relationship — it is the beginning of a new phase. I deliver thorough documentation, design systems, and guidelines that empower teams to carry the work forward.
