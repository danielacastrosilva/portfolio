Project 05 — Behind the Scenes

Designed and edited to communicate both the joy of movement and the sense of community

Step 01

Understanding the Vision

The process began with meetings focused on understanding the purpose behind the Zumba classes and how they connected physical health, mental wellbeing, and community within the church’s values and activities. I worked closely with the priest leading the sessions to gain a deeper understanding of both the atmosphere and the intention behind the initiative.

From the beginning, I was given strong creative freedom regarding the visual direction and storytelling. Alongside production planning and scheduling, I attended short pre- and post-session meetings to review material and align the content direction. I also researched how Zumba and fitness-focused communities present themselves on social media, analysing pacing, framing, and videography styles commonly used within the space.

Deliverable Research findings
Step 02

Filming & Visual Composition

The filming process focused on capturing a wide range of material to create flexibility in post-production. In content production, moments cannot always be recreated, making it essential to gather enough footage while still filming with intention and structure.

Rather than recording randomly, I focused on visual harmony, movement orchestration, and rhythm within each shot. Every angle and sequence was captured with future layouts, typography placement, and pacing in mind. To create both quality and agility during production, I combined DSLR footage with spontaneous recordings using an iPhone for faster and more natural captures.

Deliverable Visual assets, editing roughs
Step 03

Editing & Motion Design

Using Adobe Creative Suite, I edited both still images and video assets for social media distribution. Longer-form edits were developed in Adobe Premiere Pro, where color grading was used to create consistency across footage and maintain a cohesive visual tone.

The editing style balanced dynamic movement with softer transitions to reflect both the energy of Zumba and the welcoming atmosphere of the church. Variations in speed, including slow motion and real-time pacing, were used strategically to emphasize movement, emotion, and rhythm throughout the content.

Deliverable Edited video assets, Drafts for social media
Step 04

Final Content Delivery

The final deliverables included optimized PNG image assets and MP4 video formats prepared for seamless social media publishing across multiple platforms.

All content was adapted with platform-safe layouts in mind, ensuring that Swedish and Spanish translations remained clear and readable without overlapping interface elements or platform buttons. Typography, logo placement, and graphic elements were aligned with the existing visual identity guidelines, resulting in vibrant and engaging content ready for public distribution.

Delivered Ready-to-post content
Outcome Increased visibility and accessibility for community events
