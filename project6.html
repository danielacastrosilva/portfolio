Project 06 — Behind the Scenes

A deep dive into the process behind the improvements to Immanuelskyrkan’s website

Step 01

Vision Alignment & Art Direction

The process began through close dialogue with members of the vocal ensemble, where I gathered direction for the visual identity of each concert. Some projects started from descriptive themes and written concepts, while others evolved through collaborative moodboards and visual references.

Clear communication early in the process became essential. I approached the project strategically by mapping all required poster elements from the beginning, including typography hierarchy, venue information, dates, and mandatory content placement. This created a stable foundation while still leaving room for creative exploration.

Deliverable List of requirements
Step 02

Sketching & Composition

The ideation phase combined hand sketches with early digital compositions in Adobe InDesign. I intentionally kept the layouts minimal in the beginning to avoid overdesigning before the composition and visual direction had been validated.

Throughout the process, I maintained ongoing discussions with stakeholders as concepts evolved. This stage focused heavily on balancing aesthetics with communication, exploring illustration styles, visual rhythm, negative space, and graphic elements that could strengthen the emotional tone of each concert theme.

Deliverable Rough sketches
Step 03

Visual Development & Iteration

Once a direction was approved, the selected concept moved into a more refined visual development phase where illustration details, typography systems, and color relationships were fully explored.

Even at this stage, flexibility remained important. Design is iterative by nature, and refinement continued through ongoing feedback and collaboration. I continuously adjusted compositions, visual hierarchy, and stylistic elements to ensure the final outcome aligned both with the ensemble’s vision and the atmosphere the performances aimed to create.

Deliverable Early drafts
Step 04

Final Posters & Print Delivery

The final deliverables included print-ready poster and flyer files prepared in CMYK for professional production across multiple formats ranging from A2 to A5.

The designs were adapted to function both as standalone promotional posters and as front covers for folded concert programs distributed during performances. Through cohesive illustration, typography, and layout systems, the printed material became an extension of the concert experience itself, visually preparing visitors for the themes and emotions of the performance ahead.
